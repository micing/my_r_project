For multiple datasets or workbook sheets, use a named list of tibbles.
For a single dataset or workbook sheet, use a single tibble.
data$cleaned contains cleaned, analysis-ready data. Its structure should fit the project.
Additional components may be added under data using descriptive names appropriate to their purpose.
